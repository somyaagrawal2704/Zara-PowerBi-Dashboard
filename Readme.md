# 🛍️ Zara Sales Analysis Dashboard
        
## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing Zara sales data using **Microsoft Power BI** to explore product performance, sales volume, promotional activities, seasonal patterns, pricing, and product positioning.

The dashboard transforms raw sales data into interactive visualizations and key performance indicators (KPIs), helping users explore retail sales patterns and support data-driven business decisions.

This is my first Power BI portfolio project, developed to strengthen my skills in business intelligence, data visualization, and analytical storytelling.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze sales volume across product sections.
- Examine product distribution and promotional activity.
- Compare sales across different product positions.
- Explore seasonal sales patterns.
- Track key performance indicators such as total products, sales volume, and average product price.
- Build an interactive dashboard to communicate business data effectively.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI Desktop** — Dashboard development and visualization.
- **Power Query** — Data cleaning and transformation.
- **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** — Measures and KPI calculations.
- **Data Visualization** — Presenting patterns and comparisons.
- **CSV** — Source data format.

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators

The dashboard currently displays the following metrics:

| KPI | Description |
|---|---|
| Total Products | Number of products represented in the dataset |
| Total Sales | Sales metric displayed in the dashboard; its definition and unit should be verified |
| Average Product Price | Average product price recorded in the data |
| Promoted Products | Count of products classified as promoted |

## 📉 Dashboard Visualizations

### 1. Sales Volume by Section
Compares sales volume between the Man and Woman sections to explore differences in product-section performance.

### 2. Products by Promotion Status
Displays the distribution of products across promoted and non-promoted categories.

### 3. Sales Volume by Seasonality
Compares sales volume between seasonal and non-seasonal product categories.

### 4. Sales by Product Position
Examines sales across product placement categories, including:
- Aisle
- End-cap
- Front of Store

### 5. Sales by Promotion and Date
Provides a breakdown of the displayed sales measure across the date hierarchy and promotion categories. This visualization can be refined to make the underlying comparisons clearer.

## 💡 Business Questions

This project explores the following questions:

1. Which product section has the highest sales volume?
2. How are products distributed between promoted and non-promoted categories?
3. How does sales volume differ between seasonal and non-seasonal products?
4. Which product positions are associated with higher sales volume?
5. What are the key product and pricing metrics in the dataset?
6. What additional analysis could help identify high-performing products?

1. Download or clone this repository.

3. Open `Zara_Sales_Dashboard.pbix`.

6. Explore the dashboard and its available visual interactions.
